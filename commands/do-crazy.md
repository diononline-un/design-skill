# do-crazy

Off-distribution design after real research. Not more styling: a different world, a
structure, material or composition the category would not attempt.

**The test:** if the result would look at home next to the competitors you researched,
it failed. Not "failed to be crazy": failed to be decided.

1. **Research first**, `layers/06-research.md` in full. Boldness without research lands
   on the current tasteful default with more confidence. If the user insists on skipping
   it, do so and label the result **unresearched** in the report.
2. **Three worlds, not three palettes.** Each must be able to carry the whole site and be
   viable now: a world that needs assets you do not have, claims that are not true or
   performance you cannot afford is unfinished, not bold.
   ```
   World:       <name it: "the cadastral drawing", "the roof at 6 am">
   The move:    <the one structural thing the category would not attempt>
   Material:    <what the page is made of: paper, glass, light, data>
   Type:        <character, not the face>
   Composition: <where the subject sits, a spatial idea, not "split hero">
   Proof:       <the one thing only this product can show, and how>
   Evidence:    <the research finding this world grows from, with its source>
   Cost:        <what it makes harder: conversion, load, legibility>
   Dies if:     <the condition under which it fails>
   ```
   Three worlds that are one world in three palettes are variations, not alternatives.
3. **Anti-default gate** (`layers/06-research.md` step 8) on every world, every line.
4. **Show three with a recommendation tied to the research**, then stop. The client must
   choose: it is their risk, and choosing for them removes the agency the direction needs.
5. **Build the winner at full commitment.** Half-committed bold work reads as a mistake.
   One dominant move, executed completely; quiet chrome so the move can be loud; the
   craft floor still applies; no new decoration to replace the removed decoration.
   When the move fights usability, name the trade and offer the split ("A: the drawing
   carries the nav, ~1 s of discovery. B: drawing + conventional nav below. A is the
   design, B the compromise"). Never silently degrade the idea or break the task.
6. **Honesty gate**: `render-audit.py` clean at 390 (the most common casualty); the primary
   action still reads as primary; the task completes by keyboard; the page loads (a world
   that needs 4 MB of imagery is a world only you see); nothing claimed that is not true.
   Any failure means deferred, not done.
7. **Report**
```
Mode: Persuade
Research: <3 findings with sources: nouns, trust signal, category read>
World chosen: <name> - the move: <one line>
Rejected: <the other two, one line each, with why>
Anti-default: <what was checked and what changed>
Cost accepted: <what is harder, and the mitigation>
Score: 81/100 - D4 H4 T4 U3
```

Failures: glow, glass and motion presented as a world; committing 40 % (all the risk,
none of the payoff); bold that does not work. Keeping the old hero because it converts
means this is `do-design-renew` with the hero on the survival list.
